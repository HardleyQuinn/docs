EvilChat is a decentralized, end-to-end encrypted messaging platform built on the **EVIL** (Event Verified Immutable Ledger) protocol. Unlike traditional chat apps, EvilChat requires no accounts, no email addresses, and no passwords — your identity is an Ed25519 keypair that lives only on your device, and your messages are encrypted ciphertext from the moment you hit send. Relays are deliberately "dumb": they store and forward opaque signed envelopes without ever reading, filtering, or understanding what's inside. Everything that makes EvilChat work — encryption, moderation, reactions, edit history — is a client-side projection over an append-only event stream.

## Get Up and Running

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open EvilChat in your browser">
    Navigate to the EvilChat web app (or the local URL if you're running the demo). No sign-up form, no email confirmation — just the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your identity is generated automatically">
    On first load, EvilChat generates a fresh Ed25519 keypair on your device. Your DID (`did:evil:<base64url(pubkey)>`) is announced to the directory stream as a signed plaintext `Identity` envelope — the only kind that is ever sent in the clear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join or create a channel">
    Browse the channel list to join an existing channel, or hit **⌘K** to open the command palette and create a new one. When you create a channel, EvilChat automatically seals the AES-256-GCM RoomKey to every visible peer so they can decrypt your messages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start chatting">
    Type your message and send. EvilChat encrypts it under the channel RoomKey, signs the envelope with your Ed25519 key, stamps it with a proof-of-work nonce, and POSTs it to the relay — all before the bytes leave your device. Peers poll the relay and decrypt locally.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key Features

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Self-Sovereign Identity" icon="key">
    No accounts, no passwords. Your identity is an Ed25519 keypair. Your DID is derived from your public key and is yours forever — no server can revoke it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Relay-Blind Encryption" icon="eye-slash">
    Relays store only opaque ciphertext. They perform mechanical checks (size, PoW, rate limit) but never read a message body, verify a signature, or filter by content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Permanent Vault Archive" icon="vault">
    Pair the optional self-hosted `evilvault` daemon to keep a permanent, Storage-Key-encrypted archive of your message history — fully under your control.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
